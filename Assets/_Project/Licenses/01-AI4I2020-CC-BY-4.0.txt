Changes made in this application:
- Rows are replayed in order as the live telemetry of a simulated CNC machining centre.
- The product type (L, M, H) is published as a number (0, 1, 2).
- Normal running skips rows that report a failure or are close to a failure limit.
- In the "Wear tool out" scenario the tool-wear value is replaced by a scheduled ramp from 160 to 215 min;
  the other values come from dataset rows.
- Failure risk, remaining tool life and the maintenance advice are computed by this application from the
  dataset's published failure rules; they are not part of the dataset.
