# Virtual commissioning clip — voiceover script

About 145 words over about 76 seconds. Read at a calm, even pace (about 2.3 words a second); the gaps between lines are where the picture does the work. Target times are where each line should start in the finished clip.

---

**1 · 0:00** *(the camera flies in from the hall to the cell)*

Picture this cell before it's built.
Its PLC program already exists, and it has a bug.
Let's find it before the machine ships.

---

**2 · 0:08** *(the cell; the program's code card appears)*

This is the real control program, in Structured Text, running on a soft PLC.
It drives the digital twin through the same I/O list the machine will use.

---

**3 · 0:20** *(close-up of the pick station; the first test turns red)*

We run the commissioning tests.
The first one fails in under two seconds:
the infeed restarts while the robot is still at the gate.
Parts back up, and nothing leaves the cell.

---

**4 · 0:40** *(the corrected line; every test turns green, sped up)*

One line of logic.
Fix it, upload, and run every test again:
a stuck sensor, a dropped part, a robot that won't answer, a machine fault, a lost PLC link.
All six pass.

---

**5 · 0:55** *(the I/O list, then the cell running)*

Bring us your PLC program and your I/O list.
We build the twin, and you find these bugs at your desk, not on site.

---

**6 · 1:08** *(end card)*

2 Dude Studios. Book a call.

---

## Recording notes

- Record each numbered block as its own take, with a second of silence before and after. The blocks are placed on the picture in the edit, so their exact length doesn't matter, as long as block 4 stays under about 15 s.
- Light emphasis on: "has a bug", "under two seconds", "One line of logic", "All six pass".
- Say "PLC" as letters, "I/O" as "eye-oh".
- WAV, 48 kHz, mono or stereo; room tone of a few seconds at the start helps the clean-up.
