**Last updated:** [date of publishing]

Factory VR Safety Training ("the app") is a virtual reality training demo for Meta Quest 3 and 3S made by 2 Dude Studios
([company address]). This policy says what information the app uses and why.

## What the app uses

**Your Meta account's name and ID.** When you open the app from the Meta Horizon Store, it asks Meta's platform for
your display name and checks that your account is entitled to the app (Meta's store requirement). The name is shown
on the training hub and used to label your training progress on the headset.

**Your training progress, on the headset only.** The app keeps your stars, scores, badges, experience points, day
streak and a record of each training run (the steps, the time, any mistakes) in the app's own storage on the headset.
These files stay on your headset; 2 Dude Studios does not receive them. Uninstalling the app deletes them.

**Leaderboard scores, with Meta.** When you finish a scored training, your score is posted to that training's Meta
leaderboard, where it is shown with your Meta display name to other players of the app. Leaderboards are run by Meta
under Meta's own terms and privacy policy (https://www.meta.com/legal/privacy-policy/).

## What the app does not do

- It does not collect or send any other personal information, location, voice, camera, hand or eye data to 2 Dude
  Studios or anyone else. Hand tracking, when used, works on the headset only.
- It has no advertising, no analytics and no third-party tracking.
- It does not use the internet for anything except Meta's platform features above (sign-in, the entitlement check and
  leaderboards), which go through the headset's own Meta services.

## Children

## Your choices

You can stop leaderboard posting and the use of your name by using the app while signed out of Meta's platform
features, or by uninstalling it. To ask about or remove your leaderboard entries, contact Meta through your account
settings, or contact us below.
