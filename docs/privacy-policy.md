built: the Meta Platform SDK features it uses (entitlement check, the signed-in user, global leaderboards), the files

**Effective date:** [date of publishing]

This policy covers the **Factory VR Safety Training** application ("the app") for Meta Quest 3 and Meta Quest 3S,
published on the Meta Horizon Store by **2 Dude Studios** ("we", "us"), [company address]. It explains what
information the app uses, why, where it is kept, and how you can have it deleted.

## Summary

- The app uses your **Meta user ID** and **display name** through Meta's platform, to check that you own the app, to
  show your name on the training hub, and to put your scores on the app's global leaderboards.
- Your **training progress stays on your headset**. We do not receive it.
- **We do not run any server** and do not collect, sell or share your data. No advertising, no analytics, no tracking.
- **To delete your data:** uninstall the app (removes everything on the headset) and email [support email] to have
  your leaderboard entries removed.

## What information the app uses, and why

| Information | Example | Why the app uses it | Where it is kept |
|---|---|---|---|
| Meta user ID | A number Meta assigns to your account | To check that your account is entitled to the app (Meta's store requirement), and so Meta can link your leaderboard scores to your account | Not stored by the app. Used through Meta's platform at run time; the leaderboard entry is held by Meta |
| Display name (or, if you have none, your Meta username) | "Alex" | To show your name on the training hub, to label your training progress on the headset, and to show it next to your scores on the leaderboards | On your headset in the app's own storage; on Meta's leaderboards next to your scores |
| Training scores | 1,240 points on the fire safety drill | To post your best score from each scored training (robot cell, lockout/tagout, fire safety, electrical safety) to that training's global leaderboard | Meta's leaderboards |
| Other players' names and scores | The top five on each leaderboard | To show each training's leaderboard on the hub | Read from Meta when the app runs; not saved |
| Training progress | Stars, best scores, badges, experience points, day streak, and a record of each run (its steps, time and any mistakes) | So you can see your progress and the trainer can review a run | On your headset only, in the app's own storage |
| Head, hand and controller tracking | Where your hands are | To let you move and pick things up in VR | Used in real time on the headset; not recorded or sent anywhere |

The app does **not** use your profile photo, friends or followers list, voice or microphone, camera or passthrough
images, eye or face tracking, location, contacts, or payment details.

## How the information is handled

- **Through Meta's platform only.** The entitlement check, your user ID and display name, and the leaderboards go
  through the Meta Horizon platform services on your headset. The app itself has no internet permission and talks to
  no other server. Meta processes this information under its own terms and privacy policy
  (https://www.meta.com/legal/privacy-policy/).
- **No sharing or selling.** We do not sell, rent or share your information with anyone. Leaderboard scores and names
  are visible to other players of the app, which is what a leaderboard is for.
- **No advertising, analytics or crash reporting.** Unity Analytics and Unity crash reporting are off, and the app
  contains no third-party advertising or tracking code.
- **Retention.** Progress on the headset stays until you uninstall the app. Leaderboard entries stay with Meta until you
  ask us to remove them, until we reset a leaderboard, or until the app is withdrawn.

## How to request deletion

- **On your headset:** uninstall the app. This deletes all of its stored progress and training records from the
  headset.
- **Leaderboard entries:** email **[support email]** with the subject "Delete my data" and your Meta display name.
  We remove your entries from every leaderboard of the app within 30 days and confirm by reply. Removing them does not
  require uninstalling the app; scores you post afterwards will appear again.
- **Your Meta account:** to delete data Meta holds about your account, use your Meta account settings or Meta's privacy
  policy.

## Children

The app is a workplace safety training demo for teens and adults (13+). It is not directed at children under 13, and
we do not knowingly process information about children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has used the app,
email [support email] and we will remove their leaderboard entries. [Age rating to be added once the IARC questionnaire
is done.]

## Your choices

You can choose not to post scores by not using the app while signed in to Meta's platform features, or ask us to
remove your entries at any time (see above). Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct or
delete your information; contact us to use them.
