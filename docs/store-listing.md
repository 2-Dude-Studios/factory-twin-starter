## Long description (4,000 characters at most)

> **VR safety training for manufacturing, in a factory that runs.**
>
> Factory VR Safety Training puts you on the floor of a working production cell: conveyors moving, a robot arm picking parts, a CNC machining centre and a drive cabinet. Every drill happens in that live cell, so what you practise looks and behaves like a real line. Start at the training hub, pick a drill, and the app takes you there.
>
> **Robot cell safety (four levels).** Walk up to the running cell and watch the arm slow in the warning zone and stop in the protective zone. Work through Awareness, Gear and skills, Procedure and Assessment, and learn why the line waits for a deliberate reset before it runs again.
>
> **Lockout / tagout.** Clear a CNC fault the right way: shut down, isolate, apply your lock and tag, try to start it to prove it is dead, then restore. The machine refuses to start while your lock is on, and every step is scored, including the ones done out of order. The drill follows OSHA 29 CFR 1910.147.
>
> **Fire safety.** A fire starts somewhere in the cell. Raise the alarm first, pick the right extinguisher for what is burning, keep your distance, and sweep the base. Know when a fire is past the incipient stage and it is time to leave. Follows OSHA 1910.157 for portable extinguishers.
>
> **Electrical safety.** An infeed drive has failed. Put on your arc flash PPE item by item, read the cabinet's label, open the disconnect and lock it, and find out whether the cabinet is really dead: the main supply can read 0 V while a second source still feeds it. Built on OSHA 1910.332 to .335 and NFPA 70E; the label values come from a made-up study.
>
> **Free roam.** Walk the live cell and the hall at your own pace.
>
> **A trainer in your ear.** Your AI trainer greets you at the hub, briefs you before each drill, cheers the steps you get right and calls out the ones you don't.
>
> **Train, score, improve.** Every run ends with a results card: stars, points, XP, any new badges and what to look at again, and each run is recorded on your headset step by step. Earn XP and climb from Trainee to Safety lead, take on the daily challenge for double XP, keep a day streak going, and compare your best scores on the Meta leaderboards. Your progress stays on your headset.
>
> **Comfortable to use.** Walk with the thumbstick or teleport, snap turn and a comfort vignette by default, teleport only if you prefer; move between the training areas, use Touch controllers or your hands, and pick up the extinguisher, the padlocks and the PPE as you would the real thing. Take the headset off and the drill pauses until you are back.
>
> **Early access.** More levels and drills are on the way: the full lockout path, the fire hazard hunt and evacuation, and the electrical tester and safe work procedure.
>
> **For businesses.** Factory VR Safety Training is a demo of what 2 Dude Studios builds: VR training and digital twins of real production lines. Want this training on your own line, with your machines and your procedures? Visit 2dudestudios.com.
>
> *A training demo, not certified training. The safety content is guidance, not legal advice, and does not replace your site's own procedures or a qualified trainer.*
