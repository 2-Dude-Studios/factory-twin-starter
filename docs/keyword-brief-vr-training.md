# Keyword brief: VR training and simulation (M30 listings, website and outreach)

*Pulled 2026-11-13 from DataForSEO (Google, United States, English), via Firecrawl's Alexandria library. Ahrefs was not
used (free plan). Volumes are monthly searches. CPC is the advertisers' cost per click. KD is keyword difficulty, 0–100.
Google counts most wording variants as one search, so "vr training", "virtual reality training", "training in vr" and
"vr for training" are the same 590 a month. Do not add variants together. Rerun before a big spend: volumes shift.*

Scope: enterprise VR training and simulation only. Medical (~210 cluster), firearms and police, flight and driving
simulators, and sports and golf keywords were left out. They are not our buyers.

"Operator training simulator" probably counts process-plant control-room simulators and job hunters ("operator
training simulator jobs" shows up too). Lockout/tagout and robot cell safety in VR did not show up at 10 or more
searches a month. Buyers looking for those do not type them into Google yet.

## What this means for the app and the website

1. **Inbound is a niche of a few hundred searches a month, but at $48–66 a click.** That CPC means competitors are
   paying for these buyers. Expect a handful of good leads, not traffic. Outbound and LinkedIn will do most of the work.
2. **Website pages**
   - Homepage H1: "VR safety training for manufacturing". It combines "vr safety training" with the "vr training"
     cluster.
   - Module pages, one each: "Fire extinguisher training simulator", "VR electrical safety training (arc flash)",
     "Lockout/tagout VR training" and "Robot cell safety training".
   - The fire page has the best chance of ranking first.
3. **Buyer pages:** use "VR training software / platform / solutions" for comparison and pricing pages. "Platform" is
   the only term that is hard to rank for.
4. **Paid search:** bid on exact match for the safety, fire and electrical terms ($5–27). Skip the broad "vr training"
   term at $48.
5. **Outbound copy:** write the way buyers search: "VR safety training", "training simulator", "virtual reality training
   program".
   - Use "simulator" rather than "simulation"; it gets more searches.
   - Keep "digital twin" for the plant and engineering audience.
   - Never write "certified" or "compliant" (training demo wording, see CLAUDE.md).
